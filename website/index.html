Privacy

Peer to peer. No account. Nothing in between.

The iPad finds your Mac on the local network and connects to it directly. There is no relay, no sign-in, and no cloud service in the path.

Every connection approved

The Mac asks before any device can control it, and that approval is not saved. A fresh launch of the Mac app asks again.

Dead sessions noticed fast

Both sides ping every two seconds and trust only recent traffic. If the Mac goes away, the remote returns to the picker.

Honest about permissions

The Mac reports whether Accessibility and Screen Recording are granted, so the remote tells you what is missing instead of failing quietly.

Setup

Open it on both. Approve once. Done.

  1. 01

    Open Deck Hand on your Mac

    It lives in the menu bar. Grant Accessibility and Screen Recording when it asks.

  2. 02

    Join the same Wi-Fi

    Open the remote on your iPad or iPhone. Your Mac appears in the list on its own.

  3. 03

    Approve the iPad

    Click Approve in the Mac’s menu bar and the trackpad, mirror, and shortcuts are live.

Open source

Build it yourself.

The Xcode project is generated with XcodeGen. Set your own team in project.yml, generate, and run both targets. Needs Xcode 16 and Swift 6.

Read the build guide 
# brew install xcodegen
$ git clone https://github.com/Dev-Muhammad-Junaid/DeckHand.git
$ cd DeckHand/DeckHand
$ xcodegen generate
$ open DeckHand.xcodeproj
