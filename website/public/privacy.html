Privacy

Your Mac stays yours.

What Deck Hand and this website collect, why, and how to have it removed.

Last updated 9 October 2026

The short version

  • No accounts, no ads, no trackers
  • Your screen, input and captures go device to device, never to us
  • We keep your email only if you join the waitlist, and only to tell you when Deck Hand is ready

Deck Hand is made by Junaid at Mega Creations LLC (“we”). This policy covers the Deck Hand apps for Mac, iPad and iPhone, and this website.

The website

The waitlist

If you join the waitlist, we store:

  • your email address
  • when you joined
  • the country your request came from, as worked out by Cloudflare from your connection (we don’t store your IP address)
  • which of the two sign-up forms you used

We use this only to send you an email when Deck Hand is ready to download. We don’t sell it, share it, or add you to other lists. It’s stored with Cloudflare, which hosts this site. We delete the list once that email has gone out, or sooner if you ask.

Cookies and storage

This site sets no cookies and runs no analytics. After you join the waitlist, your browser remembers that on your device (in its local storage) so the form doesn’t ask again. That never leaves your device, and clearing your browser data removes it.

Hosting and fonts

Cloudflare serves this site and, like any web host, processes your IP address and browser details to deliver pages and protect against abuse. The fonts load from Google Fonts, so Google also receives your IP address and browser details when you visit. Their policies apply to that: Cloudflare, Google.

The apps

Everything stays between your devices

The iPad or iPhone remote finds your Mac on your local network and connects to it directly. The pointer movements, keystrokes, live mirror, screenshots and app lists it exchanges travel only between your devices. There is no Deck Hand server in the path, and none of it reaches us.

The apps have no accounts, no analytics and no advertising, and they don’t send crash reports or usage data to us. Diagnostic logs stay on your device.

iCloud

If you’re signed in to iCloud, each device saves a small record in your own private iCloud database so your devices can recognise each other: a device identifier, the device’s name and type, a public key used to verify it, and when it was last seen. Apple stores this under your Apple Account. We can’t read it. Signing out of iCloud, or removing Deck Hand’s data in iCloud settings, deletes it.

Permissions

  • Accessibility (Mac): to move the pointer, type shortcuts, and read menu shortcuts and dialog buttons from the app in front.
  • Screen Recording (Mac): for the live mirror and captures, sent only to your approved iPad or iPhone.
  • Local Network (both): to find and connect to your devices.
  • Photos (iPad and iPhone): only when you save a capture.

Every new connection has to be approved on the Mac before anything is shared.

Your choices

You can ask us what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, at any time. For the waitlist, that means your email and the details listed above. Email [contact email — add before launch] from the address you signed up with and we’ll handle it within 30 days.

Depending on where you live, you may have further rights under laws such as the GDPR or California’s CCPA, including complaining to your local data protection authority.

Children

Deck Hand isn’t directed at children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If this policy changes, we’ll update it here and change the date at the top. If the change is significant and you’re on the waitlist, we’ll tell you by email first.
