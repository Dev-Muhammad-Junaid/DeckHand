Pixel Hand redesign: one canvas, one dock, every Mac.

The control surface is rebuilt around a single canvas that shows the trackpad, the live screen or the deck, with a glass dock under your thumb. It splits into panes when the space allows: iPhone landscape, iPhone Duo, iPad. The look follows pixelhand.app: navy screens, cobalt actions, and dots that only light when your Mac is talking.

Layout strategy

Three ways to arrange the control surface

Each option arranges the same parts: the trackpad, the mirror, the deck, the app dock, the context chips, capture, Ask and the Mac switcher. Full adaptation tables are in 03-Layout-Strategy.md.

A · Dock & Canvas Recommended

CANVAS · POINTER / SCREEN / DECK CHIPS TABS + DOCK

One canvas shows a surface. A glass dock holds the surfaces, the Mac's running apps and Ask. When there's room, the canvas splits into two panes, and on iPad a Macs sidebar appears.

  • One-tap reach for everything on iPhone, using standard iOS 26 tab-bar behaviour
  • Grows naturally into Duo and iPad panes
  • Smallest change from today's code and mental model
  • Risk: the bottom of a small iPhone gets busy (handled by minimising)

B · Columns

MACS SURFACES CONTEXT

NavigationSplitView: Macs | Surfaces (Pointer, Screen, Deck pages, Captures, Inbox) | the chosen surface. On iPhone it collapses to a navigation stack.

  • Very iPadOS; multi-Mac and sharing have a home
  • On iPhone, controls sit two levels deep
  • Columns take width away from the mirror and trackpad

C · Modules

SCREEN DECK MEDIA TRACKPAD

The user arranges resizable modules (trackpad, screen, deck page, chips, media, Mac card) into saved layouts for each window size.

  • Most flexible; Stream Deck-like
  • Highest build and test cost: every module × every size
  • Easy to end up with a new wall of tiles
Recommendation. Build A. Borrow B's Macs column as A's iPad sidebar from 1.3, and C's flexibility as layout presets on wide windows (Split, Screen focus, Pointer focus) rather than free-form modules. If people ask for more after Deck 2 ships, modules can grow out of presets, because both use the same panes.

Components

Chips, keys, docks, toasts and status

Every state also changes shape, so it reads without colour: delivered dots turn into a check, refused dots into a cross.

Recommended option on iPhone

Three surfaces, one dock

Pointer, Screen and Deck are tabs in a Liquid Glass tab bar. Above it, the app dock shows the Mac's running apps with the Mac Dock's own running dots, and the chips show what the front app needs right now. Ask sits in the tab bar's search slot.

Small screens and landscape

Fold the chrome, keep the pad

On short screens the tab bar minimises to the current surface and Ask, and the app dock folds into the first chip. In landscape the chrome moves to a leading rail, as Screens does, and the deck takes a column.

iPhone Duo (the folding iPhone)

Two panes, nothing on the crease

Sizes from the iOS 27.1 simulator: inner 951 × 669 pt, cover 466 × 678 pt, with a 40 pt division along the crease while half-folded. Each half of the inner display is about the size of the cover, so each pane reuses a phone layout.

iPad

Everything at once, with the Macs alongside

On wide windows the canvas splits into the screen over the trackpad, with the deck in a trailing column. The app dock floats like the Mac's own Dock. Layout presets (Split, Screen, Pointer) replace the tab bar.

Picture in Picture

Keep watching the Mac from the Home Screen

When you leave the app while the screen is live, iOS starts PiP automatically. Pixel Hand never starts PiP from code, as Apple's review rules require. PiP shows the picture only: no input, and no overlays except the cursor, which is drawn into the frames.

Mac menu-bar panel

Same language on the Mac

340 pt wide, system text sizes, one level of sections. Devices show what they may do, and Disconnect is a real button rather than appearing on hover.
