Live shows the Mac's text, and the caret goes where you drag it

Mockups for the owner's request: the Type panel's Live field mirrors the Mac's focused field in real time and edits it both ways, the iOS space-bar trackpad moves the Mac caret, an arrow-key joystick works in every app, and a tap in the loupe places the caret. Proposal: RD-live-sync-and-caret.md.

1 · Live with the synced field

The field holds the Mac's text, with its caret and selection

The Mac watches the focused field with AXObserver and sends a window of up to 1 000 characters around the caret. You edit it like any text: the edit goes back as "replace this range", the Mac sets the selection and types it. A Tab-accepted suggestion appears as soon as the Mac app commits it.

2 · The iOS space-bar trackpad

Hold the space bar, drag, and the Mac caret follows

This is the keyboard's own gesture, in any text view. iPhone: touch and hold the space bar, and the keys go blank. iPad: the same hold, or two fingers anywhere on the keyboard. Because the field now holds the Mac's real text, the caret has somewhere to go. Each selection change is mirrored to the Mac.

In unreadable fields: a blind trackpad (needs measuring)

With no synced text the field is empty, so the system caret can't move. UIKit still reports the drag through updateFloatingCursor(at:). We could turn every ~8 pt of drag into ← / → on the Mac (↑ / ↓ per line). This needs an iPad measurement first: does UIKit send updates in an empty field? Hardware keyboards have no space bar to hold; their arrow keys already go to the Mac.

3 · Arrow-key joystick

Hold an arrow, then drag. Further means faster

Hold any arrow cap for 300 ms; a shorter press is a normal tap. The cap becomes the anchor and a dot trail follows your finger. Left and right move by characters, up and down by lines. Past 60 pt it moves by words or paragraphs. The speed runs from 4 to 30 steps a second. With ⇧ latched, it selects. It works in every app, terminals included, because it only sends arrow keys.

4 · Tap or drag in the loupe · phase 3

Point at the word in the picture

The loupe (OT-12) already maps its pixels to the Mac's display points. A tap clicks there, so the caret lands where you pointed in any app, web and Electron included. The pointer then goes back where it was, so the trackpad doesn't jump. A hold then drag makes a mouse drag, which selects. It's the text caret, not the mouse pointer: the pointer already has the trackpad and the picture pointer (OR-16).

5 · iPad landscape, iPad portrait, iPhone

The same field at every size

The panel keeps OT-11's layout. Only the field's contents change. On iPhone the field shows two lines around the caret and the window scrolls with it.

7 · For the owner

Three decisions

1 · Show text from web and Electron apps (Claude, Slack, Safari pages) per app, opt-in?

Recommended: yes. Off by default, allowed per app from the "can't read" line, confirmed once on the Mac. Chrome stays keys-only.

2 · Which iPad keyboard features stay on in synced Live?

Recommended: autocorrect and predictions on, because they now see the real words. Smart quotes and dashes off, as today. The spell-check underline off, because the Mac app has its own. Undo goes to the Mac.

3 · What to build first?

Recommended: native sync plus the arrow joystick (≈ 9–13 days). Then the web opt-in and the blind-trackpad spike. Then the loupe tap.
